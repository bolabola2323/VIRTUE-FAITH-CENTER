05

One Community — Many Stories

We create opportunities for people from different cultural backgrounds to meet, learn, share experiences, and participate in community activities together.

This is one of the core programs; the concept involves selecting an organization or community each month—such as one serving seniors from a specific background (e.g., Chinese-Canadian, Black Canadian, Arab Canadian, or South Asian). We learn about their history and experiences, then participate in a volunteer activity with them.

Afterwards, we organize an open gathering for young people from diverse backgrounds.

In this way, getting to know one another becomes a genuine human experience rather than merely a cultural celebration.

Our activities go beyond cultural celebrations.

WE FOCUS ON
Learn→Meet→Listen→Participate→Serve→Share

Volunteers Serving Together

50 volunteers from five different organizations are participating in:

  • Cleaning a community park
  • Assembling food hampers
  • Visiting seniors
  • Community gardening
  • A donation drive
  • A clothing drive
  • A winter support campaign
  • A school-supply campaign

A casual gathering follows the work.

The goal here is not merely to complete the charitable tasks. Rather: People who serve together begin to know one another.

And this is a very practical way to build relationships.
