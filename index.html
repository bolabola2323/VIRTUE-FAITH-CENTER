We Connect Communities to Serve Together

Connecting People, Organizations and Communities for a Stronger Canada

Virtue Faith Center is a community-based organization dedicated to building stronger, more connected communities through outreach, volunteerism, partnerships, dialogue, and collaborative community initiatives.

We believe that communities become stronger when people and organizations come together, learn from one another, share resources, and work side by side to serve others.
